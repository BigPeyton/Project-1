This is a cutoff FCFF capital-budgeting valuation. Exit cash sufficiency is an undiscounted liquidity check, not the valuation numerator. Financing returns, risk discounting and the cash-reserve convention differ; do not present the result as an observed liquidation distribution.

The scenario can change the action toward do-not-initiate if execution deteriorates and financing/credible restructuring is absent. Earlier shutdown, legal restrictions, realization delays, contract damages and lower recoveries can change this result materially.
