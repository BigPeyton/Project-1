5. Signed FCFE and the sale of existing securities determine cash. Noncash note conversion changes debt, equity and shares without creating cash.

## Preparation for questions

- **Why is your value so far below the quote?** It is conditional on the modeled recovery, horizon, reinvestment and discount rate. The baseline also omits later developments. The gap is a research question, not proof of mispricing.
- **Why do 18% and 15% both appear?** They belong to different saved models: provisional WACC for FCFF versus judgment cost of equity for FCFE. They have not been reconciled into one updated valuation.
- **Does positive profit mean positive cash flow?** No. The base has positive net income in 2028 but negative FCFE because investment and working-capital needs consume cash.
- **Would a Neutron delay equal higher R&D alone?** No. A full delay could change revenue timing, capex and financing as well. The test isolates one cost effect.
