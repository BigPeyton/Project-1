## Decision and mandate

### Student-adopted rationale

*AI-assisted wording, reviewed and adopted by Peyton on October 19, 2026: “it looks good; ill adopt it as the rationale”. This records adoption of the rationale, not independent AI-off authorship or completion of the separate locked prediction exercise.*

My proposed recommendation is to watch/defer rather than initiate a position at the September 10 price of $61.96. The standalone model does not currently provide enough support for that purchase price: revenue growth requires substantial operating spending and reinvestment before it produces positive free cash flow. Even the modeled upside depends on sustained improvements in profitability and capital efficiency. The conditional scenario range of approximately $1-$7 is a result of those assumptions, not a guaranteed trading range or a complete valuation of the combined business.

The part I would challenge most is the completeness of the valuation scope. The model excludes the pending Iridium transaction and its funding, and the long-term operating assumptions remain uncertain. That limits how confidently I can interpret the gap between modeled value and market price. Watch/defer keeps the company under review while requiring stronger evidence before committing capital. I would reconsider initiation after evaluating the full transaction economics, verifying a funded downside case and finding that a revised, evidence-supported valuation justifies the purchase price. Worsening execution or an unresolved funding shortfall would instead strengthen a do-not-initiate recommendation.

## Why defer

The linked statements charge for losses, working capital and capacity investment. Their operating values plus estimated cutoff cash/securities less reserves and financial claims do not support the reference price. At 3% terminal growth, the 14%–20% WACC sensitivity yields approximately $2.60–$3.36 for base and $5.05–$8.19 for upside. Mature margins and capital returns remain ambitious analyst assumptions; neither selected peer independently establishes them.

The reverse DCF reproduces $61.96 only at approximately 3.68% WACC for base or 5.04% for upside if the operating paths and bridge stay fixed. The former is below the 4.83% Treasury reference; the latter leaves only about 0.21 percentage point above it. These are mathematical diagnostics, not proposed risk-adjusted discount rates. Holding explicit cash flows fixed instead requires 2041 FCFF about 96 times base or 28 times upside and operating margins above 100% at the fixed revenue paths. That discontinuous terminal jump is not an admissible forecast. The result calls for a different, evidenced economic thesis; it does not rule out all possible revenue paths.

RDW/Avio historical EV/revenue gives approximately $4.18–$9.14 per share, and P/S gives $2.08–$7.62. They are qualified reference indications, not values to average into the DCF. EV uses a lease-consistent equity bridge; P/S is already an equity measure and uses the explicitly different basic-equivalent denominator. Historical revenue, differing acquisition scope, profitability, net cash and capital needs explain disagreement. Two heterogeneous peers do not establish a statistical fair-value interval. No transaction multiple is used without a defensible control/synergy/time match.

**Material scope limitation:** pending Iridium operations, consideration and related funding are excluded together. Customer-loan recovery, capped-call value, forward proceeds/shares and additional unverified ATM issuance are excluded from the reference and shown separately where modeled. Optional financing can change equity per share; it is not operating value creation. The standalone-to-market gap alone cannot prove that the market is wrong about the complete company. Cutoff cash is an interpolated estimate with scenario-dependent operating assumptions, not a reported September balance.

## Conditions and reversal / monitoring triggers

- **Before reconsidering initiation:** incorporate the full transaction scope and funding on a consistent dated basis; reconcile exact capitalization and instrument claims; substantiate revenue conversion, mature margins and investment needs; rerun the valuation and downside liquidity tests. A price inside a revised supportable range is necessary but not sufficient. The committee must also set a required margin of safety and fund risk limit; no automatic buy threshold is asserted.
- **Execution:** compare dated reported revenue/backlog conversion against the company-specific driver schedule after each earnings release. Repeated shortfalls in launch/space-systems revenue, delayed Neutron revenue recognition or weaker unit economics require reducing the relevant drivers, not maintaining the terminal story unchanged. Future releases are monitoring events and are not inputs to this September valuation.
- **Liquidity:** update cash, burn, capex and committed financing. The model requires a $200m operating reserve. A funded runway shortfall or inability to preserve that reserve moves the decision toward DO NOT INITIATE; the current unfunded downside illustrates this branch. Hypothetical ATM capacity does not count as committed funding.
- **Profitability and capital:** test actual R&D/SG&A intensity, contribution margins and capacity investment against the forecast. Lower mature margin to 8% produces about $2.63 base / $4.67 upside; lower marginal return to 10% produces $2.80 / $6.07. These weaken the range and leave the no-initiation decision unchanged at $61.96.
- **Evidence sufficient to reverse to initiate:** independently supported revised cash flows and transaction economics, a funded adverse case, consistent security claims and a market price meeting the committee's approved valuation/margin-of-safety criteria. Favorable news or an AI assertion alone does not satisfy this condition.
