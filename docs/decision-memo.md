## Why the price is not underwritten

Revenue growth consumes cash through development, capacity and working capital before it produces distributable cash. The base operating strategy has EV of -$105.930m: a net present cost of that specified strategy, not a negative stock price. Estimated cutoff cash of $2,043.230m plus $258.105m securities, less the $200m operating reserve and financial claims, supports positive equity value. These are modeled cutoff balances, not a reported September balance sheet.

At fixed 3% growth, 14%-20% WACC produces base values of $2.60-$3.36 and upside values of $5.05-$8.19. The rounded $1-$7 envelope is therefore a reference-scenario range, not a bound on every sensitivity. Neither the selected peers nor audited history establishes the assumed mature margins and capital returns.

One-way operating tests rank space growth, Neutron revenue, space margin and R&D in that order under standard shocks. Wider R&D and narrower revenue shocks put R&D first for 2030 EBIT, FCFF and value. Driver importance depends on the tested ranges; outputs/proforma-sensitivity.md documents units, bounds and causal links.

## Cross-checks and disagreements

The reverse DCF reproduces $61.96 only at approximately 3.68% WACC for base or 5.04% for upside with operating paths and the bridge fixed, versus the 4.83% Treasury reference. These are mathematical diagnostics, not recommended risk-adjusted returns. Holding every explicit cash flow fixed instead requires 2041 FCFF about 96 times base or 28 times upside; the implied margins exceed 100% at the fixed sales paths. That discontinuous terminal jump is not an admissible forecast; a materially different, independently supported thesis is needed.

RDW/Avio EV/revenue implies about $4.18-$9.14/share; P/S implies $2.08-$7.62. EV uses a lease-consistent bridge; P/S is already equity value and uses a different, basic-equivalent denominator. Historical sales, acquisition scope, cash, profitability and reinvestment explain disagreement. Two heterogeneous peers do not establish a statistical fair-value interval. Do not average these indications. No transaction multiple is used without matched control, synergy and timing context.

## Material limitations and decision triggers

**Scope:** pending Iridium operations, consideration and funding are excluded together. Optional forward/ATM financing, capped-call value and customer-loan recovery are separate sensitivities. Financing proceeds must be paired with ownership dilution. The standalone-to-market gap alone cannot prove that the complete company is mispriced. Exact cutoff capitalization and instrument fair values remain uncertain; the beta proxy is not independently replicated.

**Reconsider initiation only after** integrating the full transaction economics, substantiating operating/capital assumptions, verifying a funded adverse case and reconciling security claims. A revised supportable valuation must justify the purchase price under the committee's chosen margin of safety and risk limits.

**Move toward do not initiate if** execution misses persist or no credible funding preserves the $200m operating reserve. Review launch/space-systems deliveries, Neutron revenue recognition, R&D/SG&A intensity, capex and committed financing after each release. Later releases are monitoring events, not inputs to this September valuation. Lower mature margin to 8% yields $2.63 base / $4.67 upside; lower marginal return to 10% yields $2.80 / $6.07. Both weaken value while leaving watch/defer unchanged at $61.96.
